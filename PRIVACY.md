# ClothUp (لبسني) — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 22 October 2026 (v1.4.1)_

ClothUp suggests what to wear from the weather. It is free, has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the developer runs no server that receives your data.

## What stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored only on your phone or computer and is never sent to the developer or anyone else:

- your profile: who you dress for, style, character skin tone, and the optional height, weight, age and "I feel the cold / the heat"
- your closet, saved outfits, trip plans and packing list
- the item choices the app learns from when you change a suggestion
- your settings (language, theme, morning reminder time)

Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data) deletes all of it.

## Location

If you choose **"Use my location"**, the app asks for your **approximate** location (Android's coarse location, accurate to about 2 km). It is rounded to about 1 km, used once to find the weather for where you are, and not stored by the app beyond the cached forecast for that spot. The app never tracks your location in the background.

## Weather service

To get the forecast, the app sends the coordinates of the chosen place (a known town, or your approximate location) to **Open-Meteo** (open-meteo.com), a free weather service. No name, account, device identifier or other personal information is sent. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy applies to those requests: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms#privacy

## Notifications

If you switch on the **morning reminder**, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device from the saved forecast; nothing is sent to a server.

## Sharing

When you tap **Share**, the outfit is passed as plain text to the app you pick (or copied to your clipboard). Nothing is shared unless you do this.

## Children

ClothUp is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.
