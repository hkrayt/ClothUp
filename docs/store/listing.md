## English (en-US) — default

**App name** (25): ClothUp – What to Wear

**Short description** (78):
Outfit ideas from Jordan's weather — with modest and traditional styles.

**Full description:**

ClothUp looks at the weather where you're going and tells you what to wear — made for Jordan, in Arabic and English.

Pick a place, when you're going, how long you'll stay and the occasion. ClothUp reads the forecast for your whole stay — feels-like in the sun and in the shade, rain, wind, dust and sandstorms, UV, the evening chill — and dresses a character in a complete outfit, with colors that go together.

WHAT YOU GET
• Up to three outfit ideas, each with matching colors and the right fabrics
• A short "why this outfit" for every suggestion
• Tap any piece to see its color, fabric and why; change one item and the rest stays
• It learns what you like from your changes

MADE FOR JORDAN
• 74 places across all 12 governorates, Amman neighbourhoods, tourist sites, universities — or your current location
• Weather for the Dead Sea, Aqaba, Wadi Rum and the highlands, including sharqiya, fog and snow
• Occasions: work, university, casual, formal, wedding, Friday prayer, religious visit, condolence visit, Eid, iftar, dinner, shopping, travel, hiking, gym and the beach

STYLES
• Men: standard, or traditional with thobe, shemagh, bisht and farwa
• Women: standard, or modest / hijab-friendly with abayas, long skirts and a burkini for the beach

MADE FOR YOU
• Optional height, weight and age to draw the character like you and suit the fit and warmth
• "I feel the cold / the heat"
• My closet: suggest only the clothes you own
• Save and share outfits

PLAN AHEAD
• Trip planner: an outfit for each day of a trip and one packing list
• Morning reminder with the day's outfit
• Forecast up to 16 days ahead, typical weather beyond that

Private by design: no account, no ads, no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Weather data by Open-Meteo.
