## ClothUp v1.5.0 — Smart styling

**Colors that flatter you**
- The colors around your face (hijab, scarf, top or coat) are picked for the skin tone you chose: no shades that melt into your skin; rich colors and crisp whites for deeper skin, deeper and jewel colors for fair skin
- Fair skin with dark hair gets crisp light-and-dark contrast

**Proportions from height and weight**
- Petite or fuller figures get one color family from top to toe (or a tonal outfit under an open contrasting layer) for a long line
- Tall figures get a clear break between top and bottom

**Your look: Classic, Modern or Minimal** (Settings, and at setup)
- Modern: current colors and combinations — mocha, oat, butter yellow, cherry red — tonal outfits, relaxed fits, sneakers and layers
- Minimal: calm neutrals, nothing loud, no patterns
- Classic: timeless palettes, collared and tailored pieces

**8 new palettes and 4 new colors** (mocha, oat, butter yellow, cherry red); hijabs and abayas now come in them too

The color card tells you when a choice was made for you, e.g. "Cobalt near your face flatters your skin tone".
