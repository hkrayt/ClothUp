## ClothUp v1.3.0 — Made for you

**About you** (optional, in Settings and at the end of setup; never leaves your device)
- Height and weight: the character is drawn your height and build, and fits are chosen to suit you — relaxed, easy cuts for a fuller build
- Age: older people get slightly warmer outfits, comfortable shoes and classic cuts (no high heels over 65); younger people get more casual, trend-led pieces
- "I feel the cold / the heat": outfits shift about 2.5 °C warmer or lighter
- Weather, occasion and modesty rules always come first, and the explanations still quote the real forecast

**My closet**
- Mark the clothes you own and turn on "Suggest only what I own"
- If nothing you own suits the weather (say, no warm coat on a snowy day), you still get a suggestion, marked "Not in your closet"

**Saved and shared outfits**
- Save any outfit; your saved outfits appear on the home screen with a small picture
- Share an outfit as a short list (Android share sheet, or copied to the clipboard on desktop)
