## ClothUp v0.3.0 — Your plans

- First-run setup: language, who we're dressing (male / female) and style (standard or traditional for men, standard or modest for women)
- One simple card: where, when (now or any hour in the next 7 days), how long you'll stay, and the occasion
- Nine occasions: work, casual, formal event, university, sport/gym, outdoor/hiking, religious visit, wedding/party, travel
- Remembers your last choices and frequent places, so a repeat suggestion is a single tap
- Back button and gestures work on Android and desktop
