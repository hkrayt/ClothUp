## ClothUp v1.0.0 · لبسني

The first full release: tell ClothUp where you're going in Jordan, when, for how long and for what occasion, and get a complete outfit that fits the weather, drawn on a character with matching colors.

**What's in it**

- 21 places across Jordan, or your current location, with live Open-Meteo weather and dust data, cached for offline use
- Weather over your whole stay, with warnings for sandstorms, snow, strong sun, humid heat and cold evenings
- Outfits for 9 occasions in men's standard and traditional styles and women's standard and modest styles
- Fabric tips, harmonious color palettes and a reason for every choice
- Three options to swipe through; change any item and ClothUp learns what you like
- English and Arabic (full right-to-left), light and dark mode, five skin tones

**New in this version**

- Desktop windows show the character and the details side by side
- Onboarding shows each style on a small character
- Android back button closes sheets and goes back instead of exiting; system bars follow the theme
- A friendly reload screen if something goes wrong
- Palette notes describe the colors actually in the outfit

**Install**

- Android: download the `.apk` and open it (allow installs from your browser if asked)
- Windows: run the `-setup.exe`; if SmartScreen appears, choose **More info → Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed)
- macOS: open the `.dmg`, drag ClothUp to Applications, then right-click → **Open** the first time
- Linux: use the `.AppImage` or install the `.deb`
