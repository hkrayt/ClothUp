## ClothUp v1.1.0 — Better weather data

**More places**
- 74 places (was 21) across all 12 governorates: main towns, Amman neighbourhoods from downtown (~770 m) to Tla' Al-Ali and Khalda (~1030 m), tourist spots like Mount Nebo, Ma'in Hot Springs, the Baptism Site, Wadi Mujib, Little Petra, Feynan and Tala Bay, universities, Queen Alia Airport and King Hussein Bridge
- The place list is grouped by governorate, with icons, climate zone and elevation; search ranks exact names and cities first

**Richer forecasts**
- Up to 16 days ahead, marked "less certain" after a week
- Feels like **in the sun** and **in the shade**: a sunny 22° that's 14° in the shade now gets a light layer
- Wind direction, fog warnings, the hot dry desert wind (sharqiya), snow on the ground and snow risk on high ground
- An hour-by-hour chart of feels-like temperature and rain chance across your stay

**Works further ahead and offline**
- Pick any date up to a year ahead: beyond the forecast, ClothUp uses typical weather for that month, built from 3 years of historical data for every place
- Offline with nothing saved, you still get typical weather instead of an error
- Saved forecasts show instantly and refresh in the background
