## ClothUp v1.4.0 — Plan ahead

**Trip planner**
- Pick a place, a start date (up to two months ahead) and 2–10 days; first and last days can be travel days
- An outfit for every day, from the forecast or typical weather further ahead
- Pieces are reused across days where it makes sense — same palette, same trousers, coat and shoes — so you pack less, with fresh tops each day
- One packing list, head to toe, with counts (e.g. "thobe × 3") and checkboxes that stay ticked
- Tap a day to open it and change any item

**Morning reminder (Android)**
- Switch it on in Settings and pick a time: each morning a notification shows the day's feels-like range and what to wear, for your last place and occasion
- Notification permission is asked only when you switch it on
