## ClothUp v0.2.0 — Live weather

Pick a place and see its weather for the next few hours.

- 21 places across Jordan with real coordinates and elevation, from Aqaba and the Dead Sea to Ajloun, Petra and Wadi Rum
- Search in English or Arabic (spelling variants like إربد / اربد both work), or use your current location
- Weather card: temperature, feels-like, the range while you're there, rain, wind, UV and humidity
- Plain-language warnings for sandstorms, dust, snow, strong gusts, big day/night swings and humid heat
- Works offline with the last saved forecast
